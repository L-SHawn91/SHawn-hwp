# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SHawn-hwp to be a practical, respectful open-source project for document-conversion and QA work.

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and focused on reproducible technical discussion.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Publishing another person's private information
- Posting secrets, private documents, patient/sample data, or non-public research material
- Repeated off-topic or bad-faith disruption

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may block participants who violate this code of conduct.

For sensitive reports, avoid public issue comments and contact the maintainer through GitHub or private vulnerability reporting when available.
