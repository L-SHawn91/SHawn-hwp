# Quality Rubric v1

- text preservation: 25
- structure preservation: 20
- table preservation: 15
- image/caption preservation: 10
- footnote/numbering preservation: 10
- submission suitability: 10
- round-trip stability: 10

Interpretation:
- 90-100: near submission-ready
- 80-89: minor repair needed
- 70-79: working draft quality
- below 70: unsafe without repair
